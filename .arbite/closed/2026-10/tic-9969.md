## Description
A project moved on disk derives a new workspace id (hash of root+sink+store root). Attempts and claims written before the move keep the old id and doctor reports attempt_for_another_workspace forever; --fix fixed none (seen in diku: 57 attempts on ws-0f10, store ws-acfe). Also the recorded binding itself can lag the derived id until init is rerun. Fix: doctor reports a stale binding / foreign stamps, and --fix re-records the binding from the derived workspace and restamps attempts (field) and claims (re-keyed, since claim ids derive from workspace+path; a released foreign claim whose path already has a current record is dropped as superseded). One store per project means any foreign stamp in this store is from an earlier location of the same store.
